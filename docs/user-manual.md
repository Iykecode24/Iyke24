# User Manual — Iyke Content Studio

## Getting Started

Welcome to **Iyke Content Studio** — your AI-powered content production platform!

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit the studio URL
2. Click **Create Account**
3. Enter your name, email, and password
4. Check your email for verification link
5. Click the link to verify your account
6. Log in and start creating!

### Dashboard Overview
After logging in, you'll see the main dashboard with:
- **Quick Stats**: Your total projects, active renders, and completed videos
- **Create New**: Six studio tiles for different content types
- **Recent Projects**: Your latest projects with status tracking
- **Render Activity**: Live progress of any rendering jobs

## Creating Content

### 🎬 Creating a Movie

1. Click **New Movie** on the dashboard
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: Your movie's name
   - **Story Idea**: A brief description of what happens
   - **Genre**: Action, Comedy, Drama, etc.
   - **Duration**: How long the video should be
   - **Visual Style**: Cinematic, Animated, Realistic, etc.
3. Click **Generate Script**
4. Review the AI-generated script:
   - Edit dialogue, scenes, or characters
   - Lock scenes you're happy with
   - Regenerate individual scenes
5. Review characters and voices
6. Check the estimated cost
7. Click **Start Rendering**
8. Monitor progress in real-time
9. Review the completed video
10. Download or publish!

### 🧸 Creating a Children's Cartoon

1. Click **Children's Cartoon** on the dashboard
2. Enter the cartoon details:
   - Title and story idea
   - Educational topic (Alphabet, Numbers, Science, etc.)
   - Target age group (1-3, 4-6, 7-10)
   - Animation style and duration
3. The system creates child-safe content automatically
4. Review and edit the script
5. Select character voices
6. Render and publish

### 📚 Creating an Explainer Video

1. Click **Explainer Video**
2. Enter your topic and key points
3. Upload supporting materials (slides, images, documents)
4. Select a presenter avatar and voice
5. Choose background style and branding
6. Review the generated script
7. Render and export

### 📰 Creating a News Video

1. Click **News Video**
2. Paste a news article URL
3. The system summarizes the article professionally
4. Select an anchor avatar and voice
5. Choose studio background and format
6. Review for accuracy
7. Render and publish

### 🖼️ Image to Video

1. Click **Image to Video**
2. Upload one or more photographs
3. Describe the desired animation
4. Select duration and camera movement
5. Add voice or music if desired
6. Render

### 📢 Creating an Advertisement

1. Click **Advertisement**
2. Upload a product image or enter a product page URL
3. Fill in product details (name, price, features)
4. Select ad style and target platform
5. Review the generated ad concept
6. Render and export for each platform

## Managing Projects

### Project Status
Your projects move through these stages:
- **Draft** → **Planning** → **Scriptwriting** → **Character Creation**
- → **Storyboarding** → **Voice Generation** → **Scene Generation**
- → **Lip Sync** → **Editing** → **Upscaling** → **Rendering**
- → **Uploading** → **Published**

### Editing a Project
- Click any project to open it
- Navigate between tabs: Script, Scenes, Characters, Voices, Media
- Changes are auto-saved
- Use version history to restore previous versions

### Exporting
- Download final MP4 video
- Export individual scenes
- Download audio tracks
- Get subtitles (SRT/VTT)
- Export script as PDF
- Download complete project archive

## Publishing

### Connecting Social Accounts
1. Go to **Connected Accounts** in the sidebar
2. Click **Connect** next to a platform
3. Authorize through the platform's login page
4. Your account is now linked!

### Publishing a Video
1. Open your completed project
2. Click **Publish**
3. Select destination platforms
4. Customize title, description, and hashtags per platform
5. Set privacy and schedule (or publish immediately)
6. Review the confirmation screen
7. Click **Publish Now** or **Schedule**

## Tips

- 💡 **Start simple**: Begin with a 30-second video to test the workflow
- 💡 **Lock good scenes**: Lock scenes you're happy with before regenerating others
- 💡 **Check costs**: Always review the cost estimate before rendering
- 💡 **Use the Character Library**: Save characters you want to reuse across projects
- 💡 **Preview first**: Preview individual scenes before full rendering
